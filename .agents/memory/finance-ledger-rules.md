---
name: Mution Finance ledger rules
description: Product decisions for separating operating performance from owner funding, debt, withdrawals, and reinvestment.
---

For Mution Finance, record both owner capital contributions and loans separately from operating revenue. Keep owner withdrawals, loan principal repayments, and asset purchases separate from operating expenses; loan interest reduces profit. Calculate retained earnings from accumulated operating profit less owner withdrawals. Reusing retained profit to buy an asset is a separate cash/asset movement, not new revenue or another capital contribution.

**Why:** The user wants comprehensive internal finance tracking, uses both owner funding and loans, and chose automatic retained-profit tracking with purchases recorded separately.

**How to apply:** Keep the transaction form, dashboard, and reports consistent with these classifications. Do not count the same retained profit again as income or capital. Treat per-bank balances and loan-by-loan schedules as separate future scope.
